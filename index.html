Model: min Σ C(i,k)·x(i,k) + λ·makespan, subject to one option per task, feasibility, precedence (T1 before T2 and T3 of the same panel), no double booking of workers or robots, and the selected safety rules. Illustrative data; replace with expert weights (BWM) and case data.
